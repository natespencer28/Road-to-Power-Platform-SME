Dataverse: tables, columns, relationships

Build

0/4
Model your tables on paper first — entities, keys, cardinality — before touching the maker portal
Build 5–7 tables with correct column types (choice vs lookup vs text is the one you'll regret)

Paste your schema into an AI assistant and ask it to critique it specifically as a Dataverse model — normalisation, choice vs lookup, relationship limits. Then have it generate 100 rows of realistic CSV for your domain and import them.

An ERD you drew, plus a working schema with sample data in it.
